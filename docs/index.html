Metascience in the Netherlands

Research on research: replication, research practices, open science, peer review, integrity, statistical inference, research evaluation and science policy. This site maps every metascience paper with at least one author at a Dutch institution, found in OpenAlex and judged by a language model. Everything is interactive: hover for details, click legends to hide series, and follow the links to explore.
