History

A timeline of metascience as in a history book: events above the line, papers below it. Events come from Wikipedia and from an archive of open science blogs (debates that many bloggers joined at the same time). Landmark papers are works that the papers on this site cite most, chosen as explained below; next to them are the most cited papers on this site. Choose one of the 20 themes, or see the combined timeline of all themes. The line is not to scale: each year gets the space its cards need, so busy years are wide, and runs of years without items (dashed) are drawn short.

Years with more events and papers take more room, so the spacing of years varies. Papers are placed in their month of publication (the earliest date OpenAlex knows, often the online version); when the month is unknown, they are in the middle of their year.

How the papers were chosen

Events: Wikipedia, each linked to its article. Blog debates: Open Science Blogs Archive. Papers and reference lists: OpenAlex (CC0). Event texts and blog summaries were written by Claude. See About for data quality and AI judgments.
