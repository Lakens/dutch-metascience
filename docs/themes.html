Themes

Each paper's title and abstract were turned into a numeric fingerprint by a language model (bge-m3), and papers with similar fingerprints are placed close together. The colours are the eight broad themes the AI assigned; the names on the map are the 20 clusters of similar papers, named by the AI from their titles.
