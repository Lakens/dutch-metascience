Themes

Each paper's title and abstract were turned into a numeric fingerprint by a language model (bge-m3), and papers with similar fingerprints are placed close together. The colours are the eight broad themes the AI assigned; the names on the map are the 20 clusters of similar papers. The AI first named each cluster from a few titles; the names were then revised after reading each cluster's typical title words, keywords and a sample of its titles.

The map of the literature

Scroll to zoom, drag to move, hover for the title, click a dot to open the paper. As you zoom in, the titles of the most cited papers appear. The positions only mean "similar to nearby papers"; the axes have no meaning.

The 20 clusters

Click a cluster to show it on the map above. Names and descriptions were written by the AI from the 20 titles nearest each cluster's centre.

Keywords that go together

The 150 most frequent OpenAlex keywords; a line joins keywords that appear together in at least three papers. Colour: the theme in which the keyword is most common. Scroll to zoom (more keywords get a name), drag the background to move, hover a keyword to see its neighbours, drag a keyword to rearrange.
