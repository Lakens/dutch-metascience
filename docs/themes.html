Themes

Each paper's title and abstract were turned into a numeric fingerprint by a language model (bge-m3), and papers with similar fingerprints were grouped into 20 clusters. The AI first named each cluster from a few titles; the names were then revised after reading each cluster's typical title words, keywords and a sample of its titles. The map of all papers with these clusters is on the Overview.

The 20 clusters

Click a cluster to show it on the map on the Overview. Names and descriptions were written by the AI from the 20 titles nearest each cluster's centre.
