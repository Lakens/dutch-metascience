Inferences

What the data suggest, beyond describing them: who could work together, which themes are rising, and which shared questions could become joint projects. These are suggestions computed from titles and abstracts, not judgements about anyone's work; use them as a starting point for a conversation.

Who could work together?

For every active researcher (; people) we list the researchers whose papers are closest in content, but with whom they have not written a metascience paper in these data. "Closest in content" uses the same text fingerprints as the map on the Themes page; each suggestion shows the clusters both work in and the two papers (one of each) that are most alike.

Each person appears at most twice. A pair may in fact have worked together on papers outside these data, or OpenAlex may have split one of them into two profiles.

Communities that could talk more

Each dot is a pair of research communities (groups that mostly write together; see Who works with whom). Right means their papers are more alike in content than the average pair; up means they write together more often (joint co-author links per 1,000 possible pairs of people). Pairs at the bottom right work on similar topics but rarely write together.
