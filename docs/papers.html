Papers

All metascience papers on this site. Search them by words, author, theme or keyword, sort them, and open a paper to see which papers resemble it, what it builds on and which papers on this site build on it. The summaries and lists below follow your search.

How the field grew

All metascience papers on this site (not only the search results) with an author at a Dutch institution per year, per 1,000 of all publications with an author at a Dutch institution in OpenAlex (same kinds of work): whether the field grew faster than Dutch research as a whole. The last year is not complete yet.

Per category

By default each category's share of that year's metascience papers on this site: how the mix of the field changes. Switch to "per 1,000 Dutch publications" (all publications with an author at a Dutch institution in OpenAlex, same kinds of work) to see whether a category grew faster than Dutch research as a whole, or to the number of papers. Each panel has its own vertical scale, so that small categories stay visible: compare the shape of the curves, not their height (the number under each name gives the size). The last year is not complete yet.

Who, where and in which journals

The most frequent authors, institutions, journals and keywords among the results. Authors and institutions are those on this site (Dutch addresses). Click a name to narrow the search to it.

Results

Click a column to sort, click it again to reverse; click a paper for its details. "On site" is the number of papers on this site that cite it.

What got cited

Every paper since 2000 by year and number of citations (log scale), with a small random offset within its year so that dots overlap less. Choose a category to highlight it; click a dot to open the paper. Recent papers have had less time to be cited.

Landmark papers in each category

The three most cited papers on this site in each of the 12 categories, so that every category shows its own landmark papers instead of one list dominated by the most cited fields. Ranked by citations per year since publication, so that old papers do not win only by being old.

About this page

  • Papers are those on the rest of this site (see About): papers that we found and that an AI judged to be metascience. Some papers are missing, and some papers shown are not metascience. Citations are counts from OpenAlex. The summary under each paper was written by the AI model that judged whether the paper is metascience; keywords and topics are from OpenAlex.
  • Similar papers have the most similar text fingerprint (title and abstract, the same fingerprints as the map on the Overview).
  • Builds on lists the papers on this site that a paper cites, and its most cited other references among the works that several papers on this site cite. Built on by lists the papers on this site that cite it. Reference lists come from OpenAlex and are missing for some papers.
  • Open access is as OpenAlex records it: free to read in any way, including a copy in a repository.
  • Ongoing NWO projects are projects that list the paper among their three most similar papers on the Working Together page. Shared on social media counts posts with a metascience term on Bluesky or Mastodon by people on this site who work in the Netherlands now, that link to the paper (see Socials).
  • BibTeX lists at most 30 authors per paper, followed by "and others".
For data sources, data quality, and AI judgments, see the About page.
