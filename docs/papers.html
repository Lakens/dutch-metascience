About this page

  • Papers are those on the rest of this site (see About): papers that we found and that an AI judged to be metascience. Some papers are missing, and some papers shown are not metascience. Citations are counts from OpenAlex. The summary under each paper was written by the AI model that judged whether the paper is metascience; keywords and topics are from OpenAlex.
  • Similar papers have the most similar text fingerprint (title and abstract, the same fingerprints as the paper map).
  • Builds on lists the papers on this site that a paper cites, and its most cited other references among the works that several papers on this site cite. Built on by lists the papers on this site that cite it. Reference lists come from OpenAlex and are missing for some papers.
  • Open access is as OpenAlex records it: free to read in any way, including a copy in a repository.
  • Ongoing NWO projects are projects that list the paper among their three most similar papers on the Inferences page. Shared on social media counts posts with a metascience term on Bluesky or Mastodon by people on this site who work in the Netherlands now, that link to the paper (see Socials).
  • BibTeX lists at most 30 authors per paper, followed by "and others".
Data: OpenAlex (CC0), NWOpen (CC0). See About.
