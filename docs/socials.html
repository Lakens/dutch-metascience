Who posts most about metascience

The ten people with the most posts of their own that contain a metascience term, per platform. Reposts are not counted, and neither are posts whose only term is "peer review" or "impact factor", which almost every academic uses. Only people who regularly post about metascience are named on this page (see About).

Terms by theme

How often each metascience term appears in the posts, grouped in five themes. A post can contain several terms. Choose a theme, and whether to count posts or the number of people who used the term.

Words around each theme

The words used most often in the posts of a theme, larger when used in more posts. The terms themselves and common words are left out, and a word is shown only when at least five people used it.

Over time

Posts per month with a term of each theme, from 2020 (the month of download is left out, because it is not complete). Most people started on Mastodon in late 2022 and on Bluesky in 2023 or 2024, so the counts mostly show how many people were active. The share of all their posts is a better measure of interest.

Discussed versus published

For each term: the share of metascience posts that contain it, against the share of the papers on this site that contain it in their title or abstract. Terms above the line are talked about more than they are written about in papers; terms below the line the other way round. Both scales are logarithmic.

Busiest weeks

The ten weeks with the most metascience posts (each the busiest within three weeks on either side), compared with the median week of the half year before. "Unusually often" lists the terms used at least twice as often as usual that week. Below each week are its three most popular metascience posts (most likes plus reposts at the time of download) by people who regularly post about metascience.

What gets shared

The websites linked most often in metascience posts, counted when at least three people linked them ("Mastodon posts" and "Bluesky posts" are links to other posts), and the papers on this site that at least two different people linked to.
