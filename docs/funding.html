Funding

Grants from NWO, the Dutch Research Council, from its open project data (NWOpen, CC0). Two kinds of projects are shown: the NWO grants we could link to researchers on this site, on any topic (some grants are missed, and some may be linked to the wrong person), and the NWO projects we identified as research on research, such as the Open Science funds and the Replication Studies programme, whoever received them. Which ongoing projects work towards similar aims is shown on the Inferences page.

NWO funding over time

Projects by the year they started, coloured by funding scheme (all grants) or by open science programme (metascience-related projects). The number above each bar is the total for that year. The last year is not complete. Amounts are known for only part of the projects (mostly recent ones), so the amount view underestimates older years. When you view the site as a person, a third view shows that person's grants, metascience-related or not.

Timeline of funded projects

Every bar is a project, from its start to its end; scroll sideways and down. Hover for details, click for the summary. A faded bar has no end date in the NWO data (shown as one year).

Who got which grants

NWO grants of researchers on this site. "Led" counts projects where the person is main applicant or project leader; the amount is the total of those projects where NWO gives one. Click a column to sort; click "timeline" to show a person's projects above. Researchers with only one metascience paper on this site are hidden by default: many of them are large grant holders in other fields.

How the grants were matched

  • Only NWO. Grants from ZonMw (the health research council), the European Research Council, Horizon Europe, universities or foundations are not included, so researchers funded mainly by these have few or no grants here.
  • Matching is by ORCID where NWO has one, otherwise by full name together with an organisation in common (the grant's organisation is one of the institutions on the person's papers). Name matches without a shared organisation are left out, so some grants are missed; a person with a common name who moved can be missed too.
  • Roles are as NWO records them: main applicant, project leader, co-applicant, or researcher (for example a PhD candidate or postdoc employed on the project).
  • Metascience-related means: a project in NWO's open science programmes (Open Science Fund, Open Science NL, Replication Studies, but not the funds that only pay open-access publishing costs), or a project whose title is about research on research (replication, reproducibility, research integrity, peer review, research assessment …). These projects are grouped by programme from the name of the funding scheme: Replication Studies (the three original rounds and the 2025 round of Open Science NL), the Open Science Fund, Research on Open Science, Recognition and Rewards, infrastructure, data and software (including the Digital Competence Centres), meetings and communities, open access publishing and other, and other NWO schemes (such as a Veni about peer review).
Data: NWOpen (NWO, CC0) and OpenAlex (CC0). See About.
